Chapter I · 1897

The atelier on Rue du Rhône

Aurèle Vautrin was twenty-six when she opened a two-bench workshop above a Genevan bookbinder. Her first commission — a pocket watch for a Lyonnais silk merchant — took eleven months. It still runs today, and it still returns to us every decade for its service.

The original bench now stands at the entrance of our Genève salon.

Chapter II · The Material

Nothing we cannot trace by name

Our gemologists travel to every mine, tannery and refinery we work with. Gold is recycled or Fairmined, stones carry their full chain of custody, and each piece leaves the Maison with a dossier of its origins — signed by the people who supplied them.

Fewer than one stone in four hundred meets our standard.

Chapter III · The Hand

Forty-eight hands, one signature

A Nocturne Tourbillon passes through the hands of forty-eight artisans over three hundred and twelve hours. Yet one master follows it from first sketch to final polish, and engraves their initials inside the case — visible only to the watchmaker who will one day restore it.

Apprenticeships at the Maison last seven years.

Chapter IV · The Heirloom

Made to be inherited

We keep a handwritten ledger of every owner since 1897. When a piece changes hands within a family, we record the new name, restore the object without charge, and return it in the box it first left in. A Maison Aurèle creation is not bought — it is entrusted.

Over 11,000 pieces remain in active service.

Maison Privée members

What we hold sacred

Four principles,
unchanged since 1897.

They are written on the first page of every apprentice’s notebook, and they are the reason some of our most-requested designs will never be made again.

01

Provenance

Every gram of gold, every stone, every hide is traceable to its origin. We visit each source in person — and we publish what we find, every year, without exception.

02

Craftsmanship

No piece leaves the atelier until the artisan who began it has signed it. Hours are never counted — only the result is.

03

Discretion

We do not advertise and we never discount. Our clients’ names are never shared, and their acquisitions are never photographed.

04

Permanence

A lifetime restoration promise, transferable to heirs. Our archives hold the original drawings of every piece we have ever made — so that anything we created can be repaired by hand, a century from now.
